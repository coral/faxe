Documents in, faxes out

Add PDFs, JPEGs or PNGs, arrange the pages, and preview before sending. Follow each fax in the queue.

An inbox for incoming faxes

Enable receiving on a SIP profile. Incoming faxes are saved as PDFs in a folder you choose.

Your SIP connection

Use T.38 or G.711 with your provider or PBX. Save connection profiles and destinations for next time.
